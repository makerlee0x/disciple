# DISCIPLE: rules for anyone (human or Claude) changing the app

## Copy rule: no subtitles that explain the screen

maker cut ~100 of these lines in Nov 2026 (#454, #455) and doesn't want them back. The rule applies to every player-facing screen: web, phone and the iPhone app.

**Don't add:**
- A line under a heading that restates the heading ("Live flights" → "Pick one and climb aboard", "Climbing now" → "Biggest climbs today").
- A sentence under a mode card, menu item or button that explains what it does. Use the art, the title and a short tag (`Ranked`, `Practice`, `Free`, `{n} flying`).
- A helper line under a settings toggle whose title is already clear ("Vibration" → "Buzz on taps and big moments").
- An empty state that's a pep talk ("Be the first and your name goes up here"). Give a short status plus the button: "Nothing flying" + Host a launch.
- Coaching next to a control the player can already see ("Shuffle for ideas", "Skip it with $0", "Optional").
- A tutorial block that shows on every visit (step lists in the lobby, "How it plays"). Teach once on first run, or in How to play.
- Eyebrows/kickers over headings ("Host a match", "How it plays").
- Marketing or reassurance under a number ("Spend it on any coin…", "Your cash lives in your own account").
- Scoring or rules essays printed on a page. Put them behind a small ⓘ (see the Top pilots board).
- Loading sentences ("Reading the market…"). Use a spinner or pulse.

**Do keep (one short plain line, or an icon + a line):**
- Money facts: fees, which balance pays, a fee you can't change later, practice/play money vs real.
- Safety and can't-undo warnings: wrong network or token, "can't be pulled back", export key, delete account, loss warning, hold to dump all, public-record notes for hidden launches.
- Blockers the player has to act on: permission errors, "open in Safari", a username cooldown, out of fuel, a signature request.
- Errors, and first-run control tips that show once.

**When in doubt:** delete the line and let the art, the numbers and the button speak. If something still needs words, make it a chip, an icon or an ⓘ, not a sentence. Write for crypto Twitter: short, no AI filler, no triple-rhythm lines.

**Before adding any new subtitle, helper or caption,** check it against the lists above and say in the PR why it has to stay. Removed lines and how to restore them are in `docs/copy/subcopy-removed-2026-11-17.md`.
