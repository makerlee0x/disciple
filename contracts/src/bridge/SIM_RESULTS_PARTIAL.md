## Setup

- Seed: $77, all coin. Launch discount 33% except Dutch, which rests on the anchor so the gap is only the time drift.
- Graduation: $86,520 (12% of a $721,000 reference pool). No profile reached it.
- Retail: active $1,000/day of $5–$50. Dead $100–$200/day, drawn once per day. Retail does not count toward arb volume.
- Anchor: geometric random walk. Active 3%/day. Dead 0.1%/day. Same path for every variant in a scenario. Intraday steps follow the retail clock.
- Outside searcher trades when the gap exceeds the pool fee on that clip plus 0.5%. The gas-only searcher uses 0.05% instead. That searcher is public. The hook is generic, so these runs do not assume the launching team runs a bot.
- Dutch: bid and ask are independent. Each drifts off the anchor at 1% of the anchor per hour and resets only when that side trades. There is no max gap. Size offered is about $0.25 / gap, also capped at $50 and at 5% of TVL. The bid price stops at 1% of the anchor so it stays positive; past that the slice keeps shrinking. Every fill still goes through the bonding-curve tail, the seed guard, and the 20% USDG floor.
- Arb profit is mark-to-anchor after the pool fee, minus the searcher's own cost. Pool value lost is arb mark plus loop mark and can be negative (the pool gained that mark). Loop profit subtracts only the 0.05% cost. Retail edge is the mark-to-anchor of retail fills on the Dutch book (negative means retail paid more than the anchor). Effective fee is cumulative fees divided by gross USDG notional.
- Drain day is the first day TVL fell below half the seed ($38.50). The run does not stop there.

The previous static-anchor baseline (fee stayed near 2%, anchor fixed at $1, outside searcher only) is cited for comparison and was not re-run: day 7 TVL $310, avg gap 7.53%, arb $830/day, fees $35.34/day, eff ~193 bps. Day 90 TVL $2,435, gap 1.74%, arb $338/day, fees $26.35/day. Day 365 TVL $7,970, gap 0.99%, arb $128/day, fees $21.64/day, eff ~192 bps. Not graduated.
