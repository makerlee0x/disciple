# DISCIPLE contracts: pre-mainnet security and gas review (2026-11-14)

- **Scope:** every contract under `contracts/src` on `main` at `b1cf5e5a` (includes #367, creator-fee routing to the
  locked pool / buy-and-burn): launch and bonding curve, fees and rewards, the v4 hook, Migration, LockedLP,
  TrampWallManager, QuotePricer/QuoteProbe, DiscipleRouter, the add-ons (AnonLauncher, CreatorFeeHarvest,
  MarketLens), the USDG paymaster and the session-key policy.
- **Method:** manual review in five parallel passes, proof-of-concept Foundry tests for each non-trivial finding,
  then fixes for the low-risk ones with regression tests. Known items in `security-review.md` (SEC-01..SEC-10, R-5)
  were not re-reported unless the fix was incomplete.
- **Limits:** an internal review, not a substitute for the professional audit `mainnet-plan.md` requires.
  Bug bounty rewards are off.

## Fixed in this PR

### HK-01 (Medium): `donate` permanently bricks LP compounding
`DiscipleHook` does not take `beforeDonate`, so anyone can `PoolManager.donate` to a DISCIPLE pool. Right after
graduation that lands almost entirely on LockedLP's full-range position. The pool fee is 0 and LockedLP never
collects, so the fees stay accrued; every later compound `modifyLiquidity` returns principal *plus* fees, and once
the fee on one side exceeds the add's principal the delta is positive and `_compound` reverted `CompoundCredit`.
The revert rolled the fee collection back too, so it lasted forever: the hook path logged `CompoundFailed` on every
swap and `flush` reverted. About 1% of one side (≈ $50 at graduation depth) was enough.

**Fix:** `_compound` now takes any positive delta into that side's bucket and pays any negative delta
(`LockedLP._split`). `CompoundCredit` is removed. Test: `test_HK01_donation_doesNotBrickCompounding`.

### CH-01 (Medium): burn sell size and sandwich exposure
`executeSell` sold the whole parked balance in one swap. `_priceOk` only checks the price before the trade, the
`minOut` was a same-transaction quote minus 1% (so it never bites), and `route` is permissionless. An attacker
could push the pool ~2.9% in an earlier transaction (inside the guard), call `route`, and buy back. A lump larger
than the router's 50% impact cap could never sell at all.

**Fix:**
- The sell is halved (up to 12 times) until `quoteExactIn` reports impact ≤ `MAX_SELL_IMPACT_BPS` (3%); the unsold
  rest stays in `rocketDeposited` for the next `route` (`rocketDeposited = pulled - sold`).
- `minOut` is now the larger of the same-tx quote − 1% and the hook-EMA value of the chunk − 10%
  (`EMA_FLOOR_SLACK_BPS` = 3% guard band ×2 + 3% impact + 1% fee).
- Residual risk: a searcher can still front-run within the 3% band each time. Running `route` from the keeper
  through a private mempool/bundle, or making it keeper-only, removes that.

Test: `test_CH01_burnSell_isChunked`.

### CH-02 (Medium): `launchWithUsdg` always reverts with a USDG First BOOST
`bal0` was read after the USDG/quote had arrived, and `launchFor` then pulled it, so `left = balance − bal0`
underflowed (Panic 0x11) on every launch with `usdgIn != 0`. Existing tests only used `usdgIn = 0`. The site routes
pool/burn launches with a dollar First BOOST here (#368 gates that behind `HARVEST_USDG_BOOST_LIVE`).

**Fix:** snapshot `bal0` before any transfer. Test: `test_CH02_launchWithUsdg_withFirstBoost_works`.

### FR-01 / LB-03 (Medium): repointing `FeeRouter.launchpad` bricks live curves
CurveFeeVault authenticates curves (`settleCurveFee`) and finds creators through `FeeRouter.launchpad()`. After
`setLaunchpad(new)`, every live curve of the old Launchpad reverts `OnlyCurve` on graduation, `settleFees`,
`releaseSnipeEscrow` and every referred trade; already-settled creator fees become unclaimable. Nothing could
recover it. `Migration.setLaunchpad` is already one-shot, so a second Launchpad can't graduate coins on this stack
anyway.

**Fix:** `FeeRouter.setLaunchpad` is one-shot and rejects `address(0)`. Test: `test_FR01_setLaunchpad_isOneShot`.

### FR-02 (Medium, partly): fund-moving owner powers
- `registerTokenQuote` / `registerTokenLaunch` could be re-run on a live token, paying creators in the wrong asset
  and leaving FeeRouter insolvent against accruals in the old quote, or repointing the launch KOL.
  **Fixed:** both are write-once; `registerTokenLaunch` is Launchpad-only. (The owner could still pre-register a
  predicted token address and block that one launch; the owner is the Safe.)
- `setCreatorFeeHarvest_51` set to an EOA would brick every creator self-claim. **Fixed:** must be a contract or 0.
- Timelock: `setClaimRouter` (an allowlisted claim router can pull any creator's fees from FeeRouter and
  CurveFeeVault) and `setReferralSink` were already on the handover list in `docs/mainnet/SEC-04-ownership-handover.md`
  but not in `security-review.md`; `setCreatorFeeHarvest_51` was on neither. The handover doc now lists it.

Tests: `test_FR02_registerToken_isWriteOnce`, `test_FR02_setCreatorFeeHarvest_rejectsEoa`.

### PM-1 (Medium): dust deposit blocks the setup op
Anyone could `deposit(victim, 1)`; `_classify` required `creditOf == 0`, so the victim's USDG-only setup op then
failed `InsufficientCredit`. **Fix:** the setup op is allowed while the account can't already pay
(`creditOf < maxTokenCost`, no reservation); `bootstrapUsed` stays the once-only guard.
Test: `test_PM1_dustDeposit_doesNotBlockBootstrap`.

### PM-2 (Medium): self-bundler empties the ETH float
The signed allowance doesn't bind gas prices. A holder can bundle its own ops through the public EntryPoint with a
high priority fee and itself as beneficiary, converting its USDG credit into the paymaster's ETH at the signed rate.
The live ~$30 float empties in about 400 ops; every player's op then fails AA31 until a manual refill.
**Fix:** `setGasPriceCaps(maxFeePerGas, maxPriorityFeePerGas)` (owner, 0 = off), checked in validation.
**Ops: set both caps at deploy** with headroom over normal Robinhood Chain fees.
Tests: `test_PM2_gasPriceCaps_refuseHighFeeOps`, `test_PM2_setGasPriceCaps_onlyOwner`.

- **CH-04:** new `routeWith(token, pastCreator)` also pulls a middle creator's balances. Anyone may call it; an

- **RT-02:** `claimCreatorFeesToUsdg(minUsdgOut = 0)` and `launchWithUsdg(minQuoteIn = 0)` with a swap leg revert

## Open: needs a redesign or a decision

### SK-1 (High): stolen session key drains the wallet past cap0
`DiscipleSessionPolicyV143` debits gas against cap0 only in the execution hook (`:292-297`); the validation hook is
view. When execution reverts, EntryPoint rolls that debit back, but V5's postOp still charges the credit. And
`deposit(self, amt)` with a matching approve is allowed for any amount and not counted (`:382-396`). So a thief
with the session key first moves the whole wallet into paymaster credit (one cheap op), then sends ops that pass
validation and revert in execution, bundling them itself as beneficiary with a high fee. The PoC (real MAv2 and
EntryPoint) moves 9,000 USDG into credit with cap0 = 5 USDG and burns ~1.3 USDG per reverted op while `spent0`
never moves. PM-2's gas price caps limit the rate but not the total.
**Recommendation (new policy version):** debit the gas ceiling in a non-view validation hook (account-associated
storage is allowed under ERC-7562 and survives an execution revert), drop the execution-time debit, and count
paymaster deposits against the cap. Fold SK-2 in.

### SK-2 (Medium): payer not checked
The policy never checks who pays gas, so self-paid ETH and other paymasters (including the retired v4's standing
allowances) are uncapped. Require `paymasterAndData[0:20] == V5` and the V5 length in validation.

### HK-02 (Medium): flash pokes persist in the lagged TWAP
An atomic pump → `QuotePricer.poke` → dump costs only pool fees, and the recorded tick then holds until the next
poke. The ±2000-tick clamp is symmetric, so N attacker pokes need N honest pokes to undo. PoC: one ~$90 flash on a
$100k/side reference pool leaves the TWAP 22% above spot 30 minutes later; four flashes plus one per minute hold it
at ~2.2× against a minutely keeper. Every launch on that quote (STRATEGY by default) bakes it in.
**Mitigation now (low risk, but changes who can poke):** make `poke` keeper-only, stop `Launchpad.launch` poking,
lower `maxTickDelta` to ~200-300, add a staleness bound (HK-07). **Robust:** an oracle hook written before each
trade, or a multi-block median.

### LB-01 / HK-03 (Medium): listed quotes priced from live spot at launch
`QuotePricer.quotePriceUsd` prices `_listed` quotes (all 12 launch quotes, STRATEGY included) from 1- or 2-hop
`slot0` spot, guarded only by a $10k/side *virtual* depth floor that a narrow position satisfies cheaply; `poke`
skips 2-hop routes. A launcher can push the route pool, launch, and swap back. The quote/USD price is immutable
per curve and passed to the hook, so the coin's USD start and graduation targets and its tiers are mis-scaled for life (PoC:
a 60k USDG push fixes a price over 2× fair).
**Interim:** for 1-hop routes revert when spot deviates from the TWAP by more than X bps; for 2-hop routes bound
spot against an owner-set reference; keep `allowAnyQuote` off. **Proper:** a separate always-time-weighted
`launchPriceUsd`, and depth measured from token amounts within ±N% of spot.

### RT-01 (Medium): capped USDG buys of non-USDG coins
For a coin whose quote isn't USDG, `buyWithUsdg` / `buyWithPermit2*` swap USDG → quote with no minimum, buy on the
curve, then swap any refund back with no minimum. When the curve output is capped (a graduating buy or a holding-cap
partial fill) `minTokensOut` can't see the swap price, so a sandwich makes the user pay ~20% more USDG for the same
tokens in the PoC. **Fix:** add `maxUsdgSpent` (or `minQuoteOut`) and a matching Permit2 witness field.
**Stopgap:** the site caps `usdgIn` at the exact USDG the remaining room needs.

### PM-3 (Medium): unsecured setup op is sybil-able
The setup op reserves nothing and, with `bootstrapCodehashEnforced = false` (live), any sender qualifies, e.g. a
custom account that passes simulation and reverts on-chain. Each costs the float up to 3.2M gas.
**Ops now:** `setBootstrapCodehash(<MAv2 proxy codehash>, true)` + `setBootstrapCodehashEnforced(true)`.
**Code:** also require initCode from the canonical factory for the setup kind.

### Other open items
- **LB-02 (Low):** the crew tip is decided per sale (pro-rata profit > 0.5% of mcap), so splitting an exit avoids
  it and nets more. Charge on profit above the threshold or on cumulative realized profit, or call the tip advisory.
- **CH-05 (Low):** the harvest's refund swap uses `minOut = 1`. Small amounts; consider refunding in the pair coin.
- **HK-06 (Low):** auto-discovery uses in-range liquidity and never replaces a first registered pool. Require owner
  confirmation of auto-registration on mainnet, or switch to a pool >2× deeper.
- **HK-07 (Low):** no TWAP staleness bound; a down keeper means launches bake in an old price. Product call:
  revert (launch DoS while the keeper is down) vs. accept stale.
- **HK-08 (Low):** TrampWallManager has no minimum order size; 1-wei orders on every 60-tick boundary add
  ~5-8k gas per crossed tick to every swap. Add an owner-set minimum.
- **RT-03 (Low):** the router owner can replace `launchpad` and register USDG routes through any hook; ownership is
  one step. Move to `Owned2Step` and the SEC-04 list.
- **FR-03 (Low):** CurveFeeVault applies the KOL share, referral sink and treasury in force at settle time to all
  owed curve fees; the FeeRouter NatSpec says "future trades only". Settle before changing them, or snapshot.
- **FR-05 (Low):** a pending referrer never expires; a leaked attestor (a hot web key) can become the permanent
  referrer and launch KOL for every unattributed wallet. Add expiry and an owner clear.
- **PM-4 (Low):** a reverted postOp leaves `reservedOf` / `withdrawPending` set and only `settleStuck` (needs a
  global pause) clears it. Allow per-op settle or sender self-settle after expiry.
- **PM-5 (Low, ops):** pause the retired v4 paymaster, withdraw its deposit and stake, rotate V5's signer.
- **AN-01 (Low):** `claimManyBySigBatch` reverts whole if one entry is front-run; skip failed entries.
- **Info:** LB-04 exact-transfer quote assumption (keep QuoteProbe mandatory, never `alwaysPass` upgradeable
  tokens); LB-05 one-step `transferCreator`; HK-09 a token can forge the QuoteProbe outcome via a `balanceOf` revert;
  HK-10 the 50-tick EMA speed limit stalls compounding after big moves; CH-07 no keeper reward, OOG can suppress a
  sell (the cron retries); AN-02 `pending()` misses CurveFeeVault fees; AN-03 MarketLens is spot, never an oracle;
  RT-04/05 router sweep and middle-hop impact nits; FR-06 `tryAttribute` in a trade batch can revert the trade;
  FR-07 sybil self-referral captures the 1% (by design); FR-08 RewardsVault `totalAccrued` never updated.

## Verified sound
Curve math (rounding always against the user, Newton inverse, no overflow, exact partial fills), graduation edges
and pre-initialization protection, sniper tax table, PVE bookkeeping; hook permission flags and `onlyPoolManager`,
`beforeInitialize` gating, fee deltas for all four swap directions, no-jump migration, locked liquidity never
removable, compound MEV below the fee; FeeRouter and CurveFeeVault solvency and split math, claim ordering, stealth
claim EIP-712 signatures, LoginGate epochs; router callback auth, hookData attribution, permit/Permit2 handling,
every graduated/sell `minOut`, transient reentrancy lock; AnonLauncher signature domain and nonces; no ERC-7562
opcode problems in the paymaster or policy; ERC-6900 interface IDs.
