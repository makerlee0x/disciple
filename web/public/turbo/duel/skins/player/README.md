# Player unlock skins (original hull)

Eighteen skins the player can unlock with XP and wear in Trading Duel and Turbo Royale. They use the original in-game hull, the same silhouette as `../../rocket.webp`. Each file is a transparent 372×1024 PNG, nose up, with no flame, shadow or text. Downscale in code.

These are files only. Nothing is wired up. XP costs are left blank for lee.

Silver chrome (`../../rocket.webp`) is the starter. Everyone has it unlocked. These eighteen skins are extras you earn; none of them replace the starter until the player chooses one.

## Rarity

The ladder follows the order most players already know (Fortnite, Apex): grey, mint, blue, purple, orange, then gold. There is no pink rarity colour, because pink means rivals.

| Tier | Colour | What the tier looks like | XP to unlock |
|---|---|---|---|
| Common | grey `#A7AFBE` | One solid paint colour, simple two-tone | ____ |
| Uncommon | mint `#5FF0A0` | Bold patterns: stripes, checks, camo | ____ |
| Rare | blue `#4F8BFF` | Real materials: wood, porcelain, copper | ____ |
| Epic | purple `#A066FF` | Glowing or gem finishes | ____ |
| Legendary | orange `#FF9A3C` | Showpieces | ____ |
| Mythic | champagne `#F3DFA8` | One-of-a-kind top prize | ____ |

Plain names (Common to Mythic) are the recommendation for UI. Themed names (Pilgrim to Glory) are an option. Both are open for lee.

None of these is mostly pink. Gold `#F2C14E` still means "you". The gold on Walnut & Brass, Seraph Wing and Marble & Gold Leaf is a metal accent, not a mostly-gold body, and not the flat "you" gold. Marble & Gold Leaf and Seraph Wing were re-run in round 2 with that in mind. Walnut & Brass on this hull, and on the other seven hulls except the widened needle, was not re-run.

At in-game size (about 73 px tall on navy `#121327`), Hazard, Neon Circuit and Molten now have a thin bright rim light along the right and top edges. The rim is modest, and Neon Circuit is still dim, especially on the hauler.
