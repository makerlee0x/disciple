# Reward chests (supply drops)

Six closed supply-drop crates, one per rarity, and an opened Legendary. They look like cargo pods with a parachute pack and tier-coloured seam lights, not treasure chests. The look comes from Call of Duty supply drops, Apex care packages and Fortnite supply drops. The idea comes from Clash Royale's tiered win chests, but without unlock timers.

These are files only. Nothing is wired up.

Each file is a transparent 1024×1024 PNG.

## How they work (decided)

Crates open right after the match. There are no timers and no slots. Winning a Trading Duel or placing in Turbo Royale earns a chest. They can also be bought with XP from the start, like Clash Royale's shop chests. Selling them for real money may come later.

A chest can drop XP, a skin, a hull or a trail. Higher-tier chests have better odds of rarer items. Duplicates turn into XP.

## Left blank for lee

XP costs, shop prices, drop odds and XP amounts are not set, and must not be invented. Every chest tier needs published drop odds (item type and rarity) before anything ships. Mythic colour (gold or champagne) and plain vs themed tier names are also open.
