# Difficulty

Picked at launch, locked for good. The rules only run on the bonding curve. At orbit every cap switches off and all rockets trade the same.

![Max bag by difficulty: Easy grows from 1% to 3.33%, Hard from 3.33% to 7.77% as the rocket climbs from $3,333 to $33,333. Expert has no cap.](/illustrations/docs/holding-caps.svg)

| | Easy | Hard | Expert |
|---|---|---|---|
| Max bag per wallet | 1% → 3.33% | 3.33% → 7.77% | None |
| Creator Ticket max | 1.2% · locked to orbit | 3.33% | 7.77% |
| Crew tip | 3.3% | — | — |
| Sniper tax | 7s | 7s | 7s |

## Max bag

The cap grows in a straight line as mcap climbs from $3,333 to $33,333. It follows the coins, not the wallet, so splitting into fresh wallets doesn't dodge it. A buy over your cap fills to the cap and refunds the rest. No max buy, no max sell.

## Crew tip (Easy)

Sell at a profit bigger than 0.5% of the rocket's mcap and 3.3% of what you get goes to the other holders. Each rocket keeps the tip and cutoff it launched with. You don't share in your own tip. No other holders: it goes to community rewards.

## Burning

Any holder can burn their coins any time, on every difficulty. Burned coins are gone for good. On Easy, the creator's locked Ticket can't be burned before orbit.
