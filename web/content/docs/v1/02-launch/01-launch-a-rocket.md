# Launch a rocket

Anyone can launch. No permission, no launch fee.

```steps
Name it | Name + ticker, 1–10 letters or numbers.
Badge | Pick one of ours or upload PNG, JPG, WEBP or GIF up to 2 MB.
Difficulty | Easy, Hard or Expert.
Creator Ticket | Optional first buy, before anyone else.
Light the engines | Live in one step.
```

**Extras** has the **Anon launch** switch and **More options**: pair coin, links, creator tax and where your creator fees go.

The only cost is a small network fee from your Cash, plus your Ticket if you buy one. Both show before you launch.

## What happens on launch

```stats
1,000,000 | Coins, fixed
$3,333 | Starting mcap
7s | Anti-snipe window | down
```

Your Ticket buy goes in first. Then the [anti-snipe](/docs/trading/anti-snipe) clock starts for everyone else.

Tickers aren't unique. Check the rocket before you buy.

## Hand off

**Rewards → your rocket → Transfer creator** moves the creator role to another wallet, as many times as you like. Future creator fees go with it, and so does anything not yet claimed. Claim first if you want to keep it.
