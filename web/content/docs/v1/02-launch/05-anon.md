# Anon launch

Your name and your wallet stay off the rocket. Flip **Anon launch** in Extras.

```flow
You | Ask for the launch | white
DISCIPLE | Sends it on-chain | gold
Your rocket | Your wallet nowhere on it | easy
```

```stats
$0 | Launch fee | easy
None | Ticket
Pool or burn | Creator fees
```

| | Anon launch |
|---|---|
| Who launches it | DISCIPLE sends it for you. Your wallet never signs the launch. |
| Cost | A small network fee from your Cash, kept off the rocket |
| Difficulty | Easy, Hard or Expert |
| Creator fees | Locked pool or burn. "You keep" is off, so no claim ever points at a wallet. |
| Your Hangar | The rocket shows in your own Launched tab, never on your public profile |

## What it doesn't hide

- DISCIPLE's servers know who asked for the launch.
- Buying your own rocket from your wallet is public, like any other buy.
