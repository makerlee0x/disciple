# Creator fees

```stats
0.33% | Of every trade, forever | gold
0–7% | Optional creator tax, curve only
```

The creator gets 33% of the 1% trade fee on every trade, before and after orbit, for as long as the rocket trades.

## What that adds up to

```bars
$10K volume | 33 | $33 | gold
$100K volume | 330 | $330 | gold
$1M volume | 3300 | $3,300 | gold
$10M volume | 33000 | $33,000 | gold
```

Before any creator tax. Volume counts buys and sells.

## Where your fees go

Pick one at launch. Locked for good, even if you hand the rocket off.

```cards
You keep | Claim in Rewards | | | gold
Locked pool | Added to the LP forever | | | easy
Burn | Burned for good | | | down
```

- **You keep:** fees build up for you. Claim from the **Rewards** tile in your Hangar any time. No deadline, no minimum. The network fee shows in dollars before you confirm. **Pays to** sends a rocket's fees to another wallet or `@user`.
- **Locked pool:** your share and your creator tax are added to the rocket's locked liquidity.
- **Burn:** your share and your creator tax are burned. After orbit, the part paid in your own rocket is burned as is, never sold.

Pool and burn are pushed on-chain every so often, and anyone can trigger the push.

## Creator tax

An extra 0–7% (half-point steps) on every buy and sell while the rocket is on the bonding curve. It ends at orbit ($33.3K) and follows your fee choice: kept, locked or burned. High tax scares off traders.

On a $100 buy with a 3% tax, on the curve:

```split
96 | Buys coins | $96.00 | up
1 | Trade fee | $1.00 | white
3 | Creator tax | $3.00 | gold
```

After orbit the same buy pays $1.00.
