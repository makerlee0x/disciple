# Pair coins

Every rocket is paired with a coin, its **base**. The rocket is priced in it. At orbit the base is locked in the LP next to the rocket, forever.

You still pay and get paid in dollars. A BOOST swaps your USDG into the base and buys the rocket in one trade. A DUMP does the reverse.

## The 12

STRATEGY (default) · AI · SCHIFFY · BONER · MEME · PONS · ORBIO · HOOKR · CASHCAT · PRIORS · PENGU · DELTA

Pick another in **Extras**. The list rotates over time with interest and activity. A change only affects new launches.

## Dollar price

The base's dollar price is read once, at launch, from a 30-minute average of its USDG pool, so one spike can't skew it. After that the rocket's dollar mcap moves with the base. If the base dumps, your rocket's dollar value dumps too.
