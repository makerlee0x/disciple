# Pair coins

Every rocket is paired with a coin, its **base**. The rocket is priced in it. At orbit the base is locked in the LP next to the rocket, forever.

You still pay and get paid in dollars. A BOOST swaps your USDG into the base and buys the rocket in one trade. A DUMP does the reverse.

```flow
Your USDG | Dollars in | gold
Pair coin | e.g. STRATEGY | white
Rocket | One trade, one tap | easy
```

**STRATEGY** is the default. Pick another under **Extras → More options**. A change to the list only affects new launches.

## The Pairs board

**Leaderboard → Pairs** ranks the 12 by a score from volume, coins burned, rockets launched and traders. Every 12 weeks the bottom 2 can be swapped out for new pairs. Launch on your favourite to keep it in.

## Dollar price

The base's dollar price is read from its live USDG pool the moment you launch. After that the rocket's dollar mcap moves with the base. If the base dumps, your rocket's dollar value dumps too.
