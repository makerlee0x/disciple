# FAQ

**Do I need ETH?**
No. Network fees are paid in USDG and always shown in dollars first.

**Can I pay with a card?**
Yes. Add cash → Pay with card or Apple Pay, $10 to $2,000. It lands as Cash.

**Is the liquidity really locked?**
Yes. At orbit it goes to a contract with no withdraw function that nobody controls. See [Locked liquidity](/docs/curve/locked-liquidity).

**Can a creator rug?**
They can't mint, pause or pull the LP. They can sell their own coins like anyone. On Easy their Ticket is locked until orbit.

**Why did my first-second buy cost so much?**
The sniper tax: 99% at launch, gone by 7 seconds. See [Anti-snipe](/docs/trading/anti-snipe).

**Is there a max buy?**
No. Only the max bag on Easy and Hard rockets before orbit.

**Is Turbo real trading?**
On Turbo cash, yes. Every tap is an order on Lighter and your PnL is the real fill. Bot duels and practice use play money. See [How Turbo works](/docs/turbo).

**Can I play Turbo in the US?**
On Simulator, yes. Real money isn't offered there.

**Does the winner of a duel take the loser's money?**
No. Each side keeps their own PnL. Winning is beating the other pilot.

**Do skins, tiers or bombs change my trades?**
Never. They're cosmetic or display only.

**Can the Team pause trading?**
No. See [Contracts and admin](/docs/safety).
