# Bombs

Every DUMP drops a bomb, sized by how hard it moves the price. A $50 sell on a $10K rocket can hit harder than $5,000 on a $10M one.

```ladder
GRENADE | under 2%
BUNKER BUSTER | 2–5%
WARHEAD | 5–12%
MOAB | 12–25%
ATOM BOMB | 25–50%
NUKE | 50%+ | *
```

BOOSTs work the same way as thrust: bigger impact, bigger flame.

Bombs are a picture of the trade. They add no fee and don't change your fill. They do count toward Orders and trophies.
