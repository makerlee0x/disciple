# Locked liquidity

At orbit the LP goes to a contract with no withdraw function that nobody controls. Nobody can pull it. Not the creator, not the Team.

## What keeps going in

```tree
Locked LP | Only grows | easy
At orbit | 15.76% of coins + all the curve raised | gold
Every trade after | 0.33% of the trade | easy
Curve LP share | If orbit lands within 12 days | white
Sniper tax | If orbit lands within 12 days | white
```

The LP share builds up and gets added in batches. Anyone can trigger the add.

## Why it can't be pulled

- No remove function exists.
- Only the LP contract can add liquidity to DISCIPLE pools.
- The contracts can't be upgraded, and nobody holds admin keys on the LP contract.

Holders, the creator included, can still sell their own coins.
