# BOOST and DUMP

```cards
BOOST | Buy · rocket lifts | /art/launch/live.webp | | up
DUMP | Sell · drops a bomb | /art/how-to-play/hero.webp | | down
```

You pay in dollars (USDG) and get dollars back. On the bonding curve you trade against the curve. After orbit you trade on Uniswap. Same buttons either way.

## Keys

| Key | Does |
|---|---|
| `1` `2` `3` `4` | Pick a buy: $10 · $25 · $50 · $100 (set your own in Trade settings) |
| `Space` | BOOST |
| `Z` `X` `C` `V` | Pick a sell: 10 · 25 · 50 · 100% of your bag |
| `Shift` | DUMP |
| `A` `D` / `←` `→` | Steer |
| `Enter` | Chat |
| `G` | Touch grass: altitude only |
| `Esc` | Menu |

Picking a size never trades. Only BOOST and DUMP do. Smallest buy is $3. On a phone, one throttle: slide up to BOOST, down to DUMP.

## Price protection

How far the price can move before a trade cancels. **5% · 15% · 30% · 50% · Off**. Default 15%. In Settings, and in flight under ☰ → Trade settings. Off means no protection: the trade fills at whatever price it lands.

Every trade also has a deadline. Late trades revert.

## No size cap

No max buy and no max sell, before or after orbit. Big trades move the price a lot, so watch the bomb.

## Your bag

The panel under the mcap shows your bag, average entry, % and $ PnL (fees included). A dashed **YOUR ENTRY** line marks where you got in.
