# BOOST and DUMP

```cards
BOOST | Buy · rocket lifts | /art/launch/live.webp | | up
DUMP | Sell · drops a bomb | /art/how-to-play/hero.webp | | down
```

You pay in dollars (USDG) and get dollars back. On the bonding curve you trade against the curve. After orbit you trade on Uniswap. Same buttons either way.

## Keys

```keys
1 2 3 4 | Buy size: $10 · $25 · $50 · $100 | up
Space | BOOST | up
Z X C V | Sell size: 10 · 25 · 50 · 100% of your bag | down
Shift | DUMP | down
A D | Steer (or ← →) | white
Enter | Chat | white
G | Touch grass: altitude only | white
Esc | Menu | white
```

Picking a size never trades. Only BOOST and DUMP do. Set your own buy sizes in Trade settings. Smallest buy is $3. On a phone, one throttle: slide up to BOOST, down to DUMP.

## Price protection

How far the price can move before a trade cancels.

```stats
15% | Default | gold
5 · 30 · 50% | Other picks
Off | No limit | down
```

In Settings, and in flight under ☰ → Trade settings. Off means the trade fills at whatever price it lands. Every trade also has a deadline. Late trades revert.

## No size cap

No max buy and no max sell, before or after orbit. Big trades move the price a lot, so watch the bomb.

## Network fee

Each trade pays a tiny flat network fee in USDG, capped at 25¢ by the contract. No ETH needed.

## Your bag

The panel under the mcap shows your bag, average entry, % and $ PnL (fees included). A dashed **YOUR ENTRY** line marks where you got in.
