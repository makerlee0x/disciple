# Creator launch buy

> **Coming soon.** The contracts support a creator launch buy, but the Launch form doesn't offer it yet.

When it ships, you can BOOST your own rocket inside the launch transaction. It's the first buy on the curve.

| Rule | Value |
|---|---|
| Max size | 0.77% of supply by default |
| Hard ceiling | 7% (the team can raise the default up to this; never the creator) |
| Anti-snipe fee | **Exempt.** You pay only the 1% fee, plus your own creator tax if you set one. |
| Fixed per rocket | The cap that applies is the one in force at your launch. Later changes affect new rockets only. |

If your buy is over the cap, the launch reverts.

In PvE mode (coming), the creator buy also counts against the 0.25% early-buy cap. See [Launch modes](/docs/launch/modes).
