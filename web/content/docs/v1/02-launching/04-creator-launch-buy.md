# Creator launch buy

You can BOOST your own rocket as part of the launch. The launch form calls this your **First BOOST**. It's the very first buy, so you get first dibs on your own rocket. Pick $0, $5, $20 or type your own amount, in dollars.

| Rule | Today |
|---|---|
| Most you can buy | 1.2% of the rocket on Easy, 3.33% on Hard and 7.77% on Expert |
| Sniper tax | You don't pay it. You pay only the 1% trade fee, plus your own creator tax if you set one. |
| Set at launch | The limit that applies is the one in force when you launch. A later change only affects new rockets. |

If your buy is over the limit, you get as much as the limit allows and the rest of your money is sent back.

On Easy, your First BOOST stays locked until the rocket reaches orbit. See [Launch modes](/docs/launch/modes).

## First BOOST limit vs holding limit

These are two different limits.

- The **First BOOST limit** is how much you can buy at launch.
- The **holding limit** is how much any one wallet can hold while the rocket is on its starting price path. It ends when the rocket reaches orbit. On Easy it starts at 1% and rises to 3.33% as the rocket's value climbs to $33,333. On Hard it rises from 3.33% to 7.77%. Trench has none.

Your First BOOST may go over the holding limit, but it counts toward that limit afterwards. You can't BOOST again until the limit grows past what you hold, or you sell some.
