# Creator launch buy

You can BOOST your own rocket inside the launch transaction. The Launch form calls this your First BOOST. It's the first buy on the curve.

| Rule | Value |
|---|---|
| Max size | 1.2% of supply on Easy, 3.33% on Hard and 7.77% on Trench Warfare |
| Hard ceiling | 7.77%. The Trench Warfare limit already sits at this ceiling, so the team can only lower it. The creator can never change it. |
| Anti-snipe fee | **Exempt.** You pay only the 1% fee, plus your own creator tax if you set one. |
| Fixed per rocket | The cap that applies is the one in force at your launch. Later changes affect new rockets only. |

If your buy is over the cap, you get as much as the cap allows and the rest of your money is sent back.

The First BOOST cap is not the same as the holding limit. The holding limit caps how much one wallet can hold while the rocket is on the bonding curve, and it ends when the rocket reaches orbit. On Easy it starts at 1% of supply and rises to 3.33% as the market cap climbs to $33,333. On Hard it rises from 3.33% to 7.77%. Trench Warfare has no holding limit by default. Your First BOOST may go over the holding limit, but it counts toward the limit afterwards. You can't BOOST again until the limit grows past what you hold, or you sell some.

On Easy, your First BOOST stays locked until the rocket reaches orbit. See [Launch modes](/docs/launch/modes).
