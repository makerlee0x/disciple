# Launch a rocket

Anyone can launch. No permission needed.

## The form

Press **Launch a Rocket** and fill in:

| Field | Required | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| **Name** | Yes | Set for good at launch. |
| **Ticker** | Yes | Your rocket's call sign, like $HALO. 1–10 letters or numbers. Set for good at launch. |
| **Badge** | Yes | Your rocket's picture. DISCIPLE makes one for you, or you can upload your own. You can change it later. |
| **Mode** | Yes | Easy, Hard or Trench. Easy is picked for you. Set for good at launch. See [Launch modes](/docs/launch/modes). |
| **First BOOST** | No | Buy some of your own rocket at launch. See [Creator launch buy](/docs/launch/creator-buy). |
| **Anon** switch | No | Launch without your name showing as the creator. See [Private launches](/docs/launch/pseudonymous). |
| **Advanced** | No | The pair coin, links (X, website, Telegram), a creator tax of 0–7%, and who receives your creator fees. |

## What happens when you launch

It all happens in one step:

1. Your rocket is created: **1,000,000** coins, and no more can ever be made.
2. Its starting price path opens at a value of **$3,333**.
3. If you set a First BOOST, your buy goes in first.
4. The anti-snipe clock starts. For the first 7 seconds, other people's BOOSTs pay a sniper tax: 99% at launch, 25% at 1 second, 3% at 2 seconds, and nothing from 7 seconds on. See [Anti-snipe](/docs/trading/anti-snipe).

## Tickers

A ticker is your rocket's call sign: 1–10 letters (A–Z) or numbers (0–9). The form makes it uppercase for you. It can't be changed after launch.

Tickers aren't unique, so two rockets can share one. Check that you're on the right rocket before you trade.

## Creator fees

As the creator, you get 33% of every 1% trade fee on your rocket, for as long as it trades. See [Creator earnings](/docs/fees/creators).

Under **Advanced**, the form also shows other places your creator fees could go: into the rocket's locked pool, or into a buy-and-burn. Those two choices are not available yet. Today, creator fees always go to you, or to the account you name.

## Private launch

You can launch without your name showing as the creator. See [Private launches](/docs/launch/pseudonymous).
