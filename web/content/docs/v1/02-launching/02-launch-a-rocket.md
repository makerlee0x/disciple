# Launch a rocket

Anyone can launch. No permission needed.

## The form

Press **Launch** and fill in:

| Field | Required | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| **Name it** | Yes | The name and ticker. The ticker is your rocket's call sign, like $HALO: 1–10 letters or numbers. Both are set for good at launch. |
| **Badge** | Yes | Your rocket's picture. It goes on the hull. DISCIPLE makes one for you, or you can upload your own: PNG, JPG, WEBP or GIF, up to 2 MB. You can change it later. |
| **Difficulty** | Yes | Easy, Hard or Expert. Easy is picked for you. Set for good at launch. See [Launch modes](/docs/launch/modes). |
| **Your seat** | No | Your first boost: buy some of your own rocket at launch. See [Creator launch buy](/docs/launch/creator-buy). |
| **Hide my name** | No | Launch without your name showing as the creator. See [Private launches](/docs/launch/pseudonymous). |
| **More options** | No | The pair coin, links (X, website, Telegram), a creator tax of 0–7%, and who receives your creator fees. |

Then press **Light the engines**.

## What happens when you launch

It all happens in one step:

1. Your rocket is created: **1,000,000** coins, and no more can ever be made.
2. Its starting price path opens at a value of **$3,333**.
3. If you set a First BOOST, your buy goes in first.
4. The anti-snipe clock starts. For the first 7 seconds, other people's BOOSTs pay a sniper tax: 99% at launch, 25% at 1 second, 3% at 2 seconds, and nothing from 7 seconds on. See [Anti-snipe](/docs/trading/anti-snipe).

## Tickers

A ticker is your rocket's call sign: 1–10 letters (A–Z) or numbers (0–9). The form makes it uppercase for you. It can't be changed after launch.

Tickers aren't unique, so two rockets can share one. Check that you're on the right rocket before you trade.

## Creator fees

As the creator, you get 33% of every 1% trade fee on your rocket, for as long as it trades. See [Creator earnings](/docs/fees/creators).

Under **More options**, you can also send your creator fees somewhere else: into the rocket's locked pool, or into a buy-and-burn of the pair coin. You pick this when you launch, and it can't be changed later. See [Creator earnings](/docs/fees/creators#choosing-where-your-fees-go). Private launches keep the creator fees for now.

## Private launch

You can launch without your name showing as the creator. See [Private launches](/docs/launch/pseudonymous).
