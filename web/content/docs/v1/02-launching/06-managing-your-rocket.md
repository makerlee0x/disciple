# Managing your rocket

## What you can change

| Field | After launch |
|---|---|
| Image | Can change |
| Description | Can change |
| Socials and website | Can change |
| Name | Locked |
| Call sign | Locked |
| Creator tax | Locked |
| Launch mode | Locked |

## Claim your earnings

Creator fees build up on-chain. Claim them anytime by calling `claimCreatorFees` on the FeeRouter contract. There's no claim button in the app. See [Creator earnings](/docs/fees/creators).

## Hand off a rocket

You can transfer a rocket to another wallet, anytime, as many times as you like. The whole pack goes with it: metadata rights, the fee claim and creator tax rights.

## What you can't do

- Mint more supply. Supply is fixed at 7B.
- Pause trading. Curves and graduated pools are always live.
- Remove graduated liquidity. It's locked forever.

As a holder, you can still sell your own coins, like anyone else.
