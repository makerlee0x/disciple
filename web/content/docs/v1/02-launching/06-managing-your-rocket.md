# Managing your rocket

## What you can change

| Field | After launch |
|---|---|
| Image | Can change |
| Description | Can change |
| Socials and website | Can change |
| Name | Locked |
| Call sign | Locked |
| Creator tax | Locked |
| Launch mode | Locked |

## Your earnings

Your creator fees build up on every trade. Open **Rewards** (in your account menu) to see them. See [Creator earnings](/docs/fees/creators).

## Hand off a rocket

A rocket's creator role can be handed to another wallet, as many times as you like. Everything goes with it: the right to edit its picture and links, future creator fees, and the creator tax.

## What you can't do

- Make more coins. Every rocket has exactly 1,000,000, for good.
- Pause trading. The starting price path and the open market on Uniswap are always live.
- Remove liquidity after orbit. It is locked forever in the Uniswap v4 LP.

As a holder, you can still sell your own coins, like anyone else.
