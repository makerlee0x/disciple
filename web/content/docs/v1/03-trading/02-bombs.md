# Bombs and price impact

Every DUMP drops a bomb. Its size depends on how hard the sell moves the price, not on its dollar size.

**Price impact** is how far one trade moves the price, as a percentage. A $50 sell on a $10K rocket can hit harder than a $5,000 sell on a $10M rocket.

| Bomb | Price impact |
|---|---|
| **PUFF** | under 2% |
| **GRENADE** | 2–5% |
| **BUNKER** | 5–12% |
| **CRUISE** | 12–25% |
| **NUKE** | 25–50% |
| **ATOM** | 50% or more |

Price impact is measured on-chain, at full precision, for every trade. Every BOOST shows thrust the same way: the bigger its impact, the bigger the flame.

## Why it matters

- **For you:** a big bomb means you're moving the price a lot. You'll get a worse average price than the one on screen.
- **For the crew:** bombs land on everyone aboard. They're visible, live, in the flight view.
- **For your profile:** bombs count toward achievements and identities such as Bomber and Nuker.

## Bombs are display only

Bomb tiers are a game layer on top of the trade. They don't add fees or change your fill.
