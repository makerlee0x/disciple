# Missions and XP

XP is how you level up. Missions pay it and milestones pay it. Levels, prestige and trophies unlock new looks for your rocket. XP is off-chain, has no cash value and is never a token.

## Rocket looks

Your rocket's hull and engine flare are earned, never bought. Pick them in the Hangar under **Your rocket**. They are cosmetic only and never change prices, fills or odds.

| Look | How you get it |
|---|---|
| Chrome hull, Classic flare | Every pilot starts with these |
| Plasma flare | Reach level 5 |
| Matte hull | Reach level 10 |
| Violet hull | Reach level 25 |
| Gold hull | Reach Prestige 1 |
| Toxic flare | Earn the Outer Space trophy |
| Neon flare | Earn the Final Push trophy |

A level unlock stays unlocked after you prestige.

## Missions

Everyone gets the same missions, so you can compare notes in chat.

- **Daily:** 3 missions, new every day at 00:00 UTC.
- **Weekly:** 3 missions, new every Monday at 00:00 UTC.

| Daily mission | XP |
|---|---|
| Boost 3 different rockets | 150 |
| Boost $50 in total | 100 |
| Ride a rocket up one tier | 100 |
| Boost a rocket launched in the last hour | 100 |
| Drop a bomb | 75 |
| Boost an Easy, a Hard and an Expert rocket | 200 |

| Weekly mission | XP |
|---|---|
| Boost 10 different rockets | 600 |
| Stay aboard one rocket for 24 hours | 500 |
| Launch a rocket | 750 |
| Be aboard when a rocket reaches orbit | 1,000 |

Missions are checked from on-chain trades, so there's nothing to claim. XP shows up a minute or two after the trade that finished it.

## Milestones

Milestones are one-time trophies. Each pays XP once, by rarity:

| Rarity | XP |
|---|---|
| Common | 100 |
| Rare | 250 |
| Epic | 600 |
| Legendary | 1,500 |
| Mythic | 4,000 |

Your first launch pays 500. See them all on the Trophies tab.

## Level and prestige

Levels run from 1 to 50. Each level needs 40 XP more than the last, starting at 200 XP for level 2. An active pilot reaches level 50 in about 12 weeks.

At level 50 you can **prestige**. Your level goes back to 1, and your prestige badge goes up. Prestige 0 is **Normie**. Prestige 1 unlocks the Gold hull.

## Fair play

XP is for real play. The cheapest way to finish a mission is to play it.

- A boost counts only at **$5 or more**.
- Boosts on rockets you launched don't count toward boost missions.
- A boost you sell again within 2 minutes doesn't count.
- Staying aboard needs **$25 or more** held. Any dump restarts the clock.
- Bombs count only on rockets you've held for at least 10 minutes.
- Each mission pays once per day or week.

Wash trading and self-trades earn nothing, and XP earned that way can be removed.
