# Achievements and points

## Achievements: the Choir of Deeds

There are **43 achievements** in **8 categories**:

| Category | For |
|---|---|
| Flight | Altitude and graduations you've flown |
| Crew | Joining and backing crews |
| Bombs | Bombs you've dropped, from First Grenade to Atom Bomber |
| Conviction | Holding through drawdowns |
| Launcher | Rockets you've launched |
| Trencher | Fast, active trading |
| Seasonal | Season goals |
| Tramps & Walls | Filled limit orders |

Each one is locked, in progress or unlocked. Every achievement has a rarity: **Common, Rare, Epic, Legendary or Mythic**. Rarer achievements carry rarer frames. Rocket parts use the same scale.

More altitude achievements are planned.

## rHALO points

**rHALO** are off-chain points. You earn them from achievements, season goals and filled tramps and walls (1 point per $1 filled). They have no on-chain value and are not a token.

### Hold bonus

> **Coming soon: not yet applied to points.**

Holding a rocket in profit multiplies the points you earn, up to **×2.5**. It's a points multiplier only. It is never money, never a share of fees and never yield.

- **Bigger profit, bigger multiplier.** It grows with your unrealized profit and tapers off, so it tops out at ×2.5.
- **Hold to ramp up.** It builds over 24 hours of continuous holding.
- **Selling lowers it.** A partial DUMP scales it down. A full exit resets it.
- **Minimum position.** Positions under $25 don't earn a bonus.
- **Snapshots.** It's measured at random times through the day, not continuously.

## Season ranks

Your season rank comes from your rHALO points for the season:

| Rank | Points |
|---|---|
| Unranked | 0 |
| Novice | 100 |
| Ascendant | 500 |
| Blessed | 2,000 |
| Anointed | 8,000 |
| Glorified | 25,000 |
| Divine | 80,000 |

Your profile shows your season rank. Season length and reset rules are TBD and will be announced before Season 1 starts.

## Level and prestige

You also earn XP. Levels run from 1 to 50, then you prestige. Prestige 0 is **Pilgrim**.

## Fair play

rHALO is meant for real play. We may review points for wash trading, self-trades or dust farming, and points earned that way can be removed.
