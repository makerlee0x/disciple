# Identities

Your playstyle earns a tag. Tags are worked out from your trades and may change as you play. Your profile shows a primary identity and up to three more.

| Identity | Earned by |
|---|---|
| **Believer** | Average hold of 12h or more, and holding through a 35%+ drawdown |
| **Diamond Hands** | Holding through a 50%+ drawdown, still open |
| **Trencher** | Average hold of 8 minutes or less, and 40+ trades |
| **Sniper** | 35%+ of your BOOSTs within 2 minutes of launch (min. 5) |
| **Degen** | 15+ different rockets and 60+ trades |
| **Whale** | Top 1% by trade size |
| **Smart Money** | Top 10% ROI with 10+ closed trades |
| **Bomber** | 8+ bombs of Grenade size or bigger |
| **Nuker** | 2+ Nukes or Atoms |
| **Launcher** | 1+ rocket launched |
| **Dev Supporter** | 1+ launched rocket that graduated |
| **Crew Captain** | Top booster on 2+ graduated rockets |
| **Gate Keeper** | 3+ BOOSTs that helped a rocket reach Outer Space |
| **Kingmaker** | Early BOOSTs on 2+ rockets that reached Morning Stars or higher |
| **Farmer** | 5,000+ points with realized PnL of $50 or less |
| **Paper Hands** | 5+ DUMPs within 5 minutes on dips of 8% or less |
| **Exit Liquidity** | 3+ BOOSTs near a local high, then a 40%+ drawdown |
| **Bag Holder** | Open position down 50%+ for 24h+ |
| **Jeet** | 3+ DUMPs within 10 minutes after a 1.5× pump |

No match yet? You're a **Pilgrim**.

All tags are public, including Paper Hands, Exit Liquidity, Bag Holder and Jeet. They show on your profile and next to your name on the leaderboard.
