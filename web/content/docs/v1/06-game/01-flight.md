# Flight

Open any rocket to fly it. The chart becomes the sky.

## What you see

- **The rocket.** Its height is its **altitude**: how high its value has climbed. Higher means it is worth more.
- **The sky.** Each named tier has its own sky, from Launch Pad up to Seventh Heaven. See [Altitude and tiers](/docs/game/tiers).
- **BOOSTs** as thrust. The bigger a BOOST's price impact, the bigger the flame.
- **DUMPs** as bombs, live, for everyone aboard. See [Bombs](/docs/trading/bombs).
- **Wingmen.** Other pilots aboard fly alongside you, up to 12 at a time.
- **The HUD.** Altitude and distance to orbit, the live chart, your bag and PnL, flight objectives, and the BOOST and DUMP controls.
- **The crew feed.** Live trades and crew chat. See [Crew](/docs/game/crew).

On a phone, one throttle does both: slide up to BOOST, down to DUMP, or tap to BOOST your selected amount.

## Controls

| Key | Action |
|---|---|
| `Space` | BOOST the selected amount |
| `Shift` | DUMP the selected share |
| `1`–`4` | Pick a BOOST amount (picks only, never trades) |
| `Z` `X` `C` `V` | Pick 10 / 25 / 50 / 100% to DUMP (picks only, never trades) |
| `A` `D` or `←` `→` | Steer (the rocket re-centres after 1.5s without input) |
| `Enter` | Chat with the crew |
| `G` | Touch grass: a calm, altitude-only view |
| `Esc` | Menu: settings, sound and keys |

## The Lobby

The Lobby is the home page. It lists live rockets. Sort by newest, climbing, busiest, most crew, near orbit (at least 60% of the way to the open market on Uniswap) or in orbit, and filter by difficulty.

**Verified** rockets show a blue check. The host asks for it from Host earnings, the team checks who's behind the rocket, and approved rockets get the check everywhere.

## The game never touches the market

Flight is a view of real trades. Steering, wingmen, skies and effects never change price, fills or odds.
