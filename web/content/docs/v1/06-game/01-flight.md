# Flight

Open any rocket to fly it. The chart becomes the sky.

## What you see

- **The rocket.** Its height is its **altitude**: how high its value has climbed. Higher means it is worth more.
- **The sky.** Each named tier has its own sky, from Launch Pad up to Seventh Heaven. See [Altitude and tiers](/docs/game/tiers).
- **BOOSTs** as thrust. The bigger a BOOST's price impact, the bigger the flame.
- **DUMPs** as bombs, live, for everyone aboard. See [Bombs](/docs/trading/bombs).
- **The HUD.** Market cap, 24h change, your position and PnL, the BOOST and DUMP controls.
- **The crew panel.** Who's aboard, King of the Hill, crew chat. See [Crew](/docs/game/crew).
- **Tramps and walls** on rockets that have reached orbit, drawn as lines on the sky. Reaching orbit means the starting price path is finished and the rocket trades on the open market on Uniswap.

## Controls

| Key | Action |
|---|---|
| `Space` | BOOST |
| `Shift` | DUMP |
| `1`–`6` | BOOST a preset instantly |
| `Z` `X` `C` `V` | Select 10 / 25 / 50 / 100% to DUMP |
| `A` `D` or `←` `→` | Steer (the rocket re-centres after 1.5s without input) |
| `H` | Switch the side panel between chat and holders |
| `O` | Show or hide orbs |
| `G` | Touch grass: a calm, market-cap-only view |

## The Launchpad

The Launchpad is the home page. It lists rockets, with filters such as trending, new, near orbit (at least 60% of the way to the open market on Uniswap), reached orbit, and verified.

**Verified** rockets show a checkmark. The team reviews a rocket before granting the mark. For now, the checkmark is a preview and uses sample stats. [CONFIRM: lee — the code names the Verified tiers Blessed, Anointed and Glorified, which clash with the season ranks (Blessed, Anointed, Glorified, Divine). Pick new tier names or drop tiers.]

## The game never touches the market

Flight is a view of real trades. Steering, orbs, skies and effects never change price, fills or odds.
