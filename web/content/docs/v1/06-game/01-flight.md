# Flight

Open any rocket to fly it. The chart becomes the sky.

## What you see

- **The rocket.** Its height is the market cap: its **altitude**. Higher is better.
- **The sky.** Each altitude tier has its own sky, from Launch Pad up to Seventh Heaven. See [Altitude and tiers](/docs/game/tiers).
- **BOOSTs** as thrust. The bigger a BOOST's price impact, the bigger the flame.
- **DUMPs** as bombs, live, for everyone aboard. See [Bombs](/docs/trading/bombs).
- **The HUD.** Market cap, 24h change, your position and PnL, the BOOST and DUMP controls.
- **The crew panel.** Who's aboard, King of the Hill, crew chat. See [Crew](/docs/game/crew).
- **Tramps and walls** on graduated rockets, drawn as lines on the sky.

## Controls

| Key | Action |
|---|---|
| `Space` | BOOST |
| `Shift` | DUMP |
| `1`–`6` | BOOST a preset instantly |
| `Z` `X` `C` `V` | Select 10 / 25 / 50 / 100% to DUMP |
| `A` `D` or `←` `→` | Steer (the rocket re-centres after 1.5s without input) |
| `H` | Switch the side panel between chat and holders |
| `O` | Show or hide orbs |
| `G` | Touch grass: a calm, market-cap-only view |

## The Launchpad

The Launchpad is the home page. It lists rockets, with filters such as trending, new, near orbit (close to graduation), graduated and verified.

**Verified** rockets show a checkmark. Verification is off-chain and checked by the team. [CONFIRM: SITE + lee — the verification criteria; the current thresholds are a proposal on sample stats.]

## The game never touches the market

Flight is a view of real trades. Steering, orbs, skies and effects never change price, fills or odds.
