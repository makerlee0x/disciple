# Risks

Read this before you trade. Nothing in these docs is financial advice.

## Market risk

- **Rockets are volatile.** Most new coins lose value. A rocket can fall to near zero.
- **You can lose everything you put in.**
- **Price impact.** Large trades on small rockets move the price a lot. You'll get a worse average price.
- **Creators and early buyers can sell.** Locked liquidity stops anyone from pulling the pool. It doesn't stop holders, including the creator, from selling their coins.

## Base risk

Your rocket is priced in its base. If the base falls, the rocket's dollar value falls with it, even if no one sells the rocket. USDG is assumed to hold its $1 peg.

## Smart-contract risk

The contracts are not audited by a third party. Bugs are possible. See [Contracts and audits](/docs/safety).

## Admin risk

Admin controls are held by the team during testnet, with no timelock. See [Admin controls](/docs/safety/admin).

## Oracle and data risk

- The base's dollar price is set once at launch. The rocket's dollar market cap moves with the base after that.
- Altitude, tiers and bombs are display signals. A single trade can move them.
- Some screens use sample data during testnet.

## Account risk

Your account is a smart account controlled by your login. Keep your login secure. Anyone with access to it can trade and withdraw.

## Testnet

Testnet tokens have no value. Contracts may be redeployed. Don't send real funds to testnet addresses.
