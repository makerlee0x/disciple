# Risks

Read this before you put money in. Nothing here is financial advice.

## You can lose it all

- Most new coins go to zero. A rocket can too.
- Big trades on small rockets move the price hard.
- Locked LP stops a pull. It doesn't stop holders, the creator included, from selling.
- A rocket is priced in its pair coin. If the pair coin dumps, the rocket's dollar value dumps with it.

## Turbo is leverage

Up to 50×. At 50× a 0.78% BTC move against you is a NUKE. Turbo cash sits on Lighter, a separate exchange, so its risks apply too.

## Code and admin

Contracts can have bugs. The Team's 2-of-3 Safe holds the settings listed in [Contracts and admin](/docs/safety), with no timelock. USDG and USDC are assumed to hold $1.

## Your account

Your wallet is controlled by your login. Turn on two-factor in Settings. Anyone with your login or your exported key can trade and withdraw.
