# Your account

## Sign in

Email, Google, X or a wallet. Sign-in runs on Privy. Your first sign-in makes a wallet for you, and that wallet is your account.

| | |
|---|---|
| Who holds your money | Your wallet. DISCIPLE never moves it into a wallet of its own. |
| Wallet pop-ups | None. Trades are one tap and sign in the background. |
| Gas | None on trades. You never need ETH. |
| Export key | Settings → Security. Anyone with this key controls your money. |
| Two-factor | Settings → Security: authenticator app or passkey. |

## Your Hangar

Your profile: rockets, level, skins and recent trades. Edit your picture, banner, display name, `@username` and bio from there.

- **Username:** 3–22 characters, lowercase letters, numbers and `_`. One per account.
- **Rewards:** creator fees waiting to be claimed. Also in the account menu.
- **Invite:** your `/r/` link, on your profile and in the account menu.

## The Twelve

Hold one of **The Twelve** (Ethereum, 12 Access Keys on OpenSea) and your profile shows a FOUNDER chip.
