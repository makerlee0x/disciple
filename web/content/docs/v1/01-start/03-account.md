# Your account

## Sign in

Email, Google, Apple or X. Your first sign-in makes a wallet for you, and that wallet is your account. Sign-in runs on Privy.

```stats
0 | Wallet pop-ups | easy
$0 | Gas on trades | easy
1 tap | Per trade | gold
```

| | |
|---|---|
| Who holds your money | Your wallet. DISCIPLE never moves it into a wallet of its own. |
| Export key | Settings → Security. Anyone with this key controls your money. |
| Two-factor | Settings → Security: authenticator app or passkey. |

## Your Hangar

Your profile: rockets, level, skins and recent trades. Edit your picture, banner, display name, `@username` and bio from here.

```cards
Hangar value | Everything you own | | | white
Cash | USDG | | | gold
Turbo cash | Duel + Climb | | | cyan
Rewards | Creator fees to claim | | | champagne
```

- The pill at the top of every page shows your Hangar value: Cash + your rockets + Turbo cash.
- **Username:** 3–22 characters, lowercase letters, numbers and `_`. One per account.
- **Invite:** your `/r/` link, on your profile and in the account menu.

## The Twelve

Hold one of **The Twelve** (Ethereum, 12 Access Keys on OpenSea) and your profile shows a FOUNDER chip.
