# Quick start

```steps
Sign in | Email, Google, X or a wallet.
Add cash | Send USDG on Robinhood Chain. No ETH needed.
Pick a mode | Fly a live rocket, launch your own, or Duel.
BOOST | `Space` buys. Smallest buy is $3.
DUMP | `Shift` sells. Take profit or take the L.
```

## No money yet?

[Daily mission](/docs/daily) runs on practice cash. Bot duels are free too. Neither touches your wallet.
