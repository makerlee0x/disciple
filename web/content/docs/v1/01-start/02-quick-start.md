# Quick start

```steps
Sign in | Email, Google, Apple or X.
Add cash | Card, Apple Pay or USDG. No ETH needed.
Pick a mode | Launch, Duel, Climb or Daily mission.
BOOST | `Space` buys.
DUMP | `Shift` sells.
```

## The keys

```keys
1 2 3 4 | Pick a buy size | up
Space | BOOST | up
Z X C V | Pick a sell size | down
Shift | DUMP | down
```

Picking a size never trades. Only BOOST and DUMP do. On a phone it's one throttle: slide up to BOOST, down to DUMP.

## No money yet?

[Daily mission](/docs/daily) runs on practice cash, and bot duels are free. Neither touches your wallet.
