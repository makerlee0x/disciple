# Cash and Turbo cash

You have two balances. The pill at the top shows them added together. Tap it to see both.

```stats
Cash | USDG · Robinhood Chain | gold
Turbo cash | Your Lighter account | pink
```

| | Cash | Turbo cash |
|---|---|---|
| Used for | Launching and trading rockets | Duel and Climb |
| Lives in | Your wallet on Robinhood Chain | Your own account on Lighter |
| Counted in | USDG (1 USDG = $1) | USDC (1 USDC = $1) |

## Add cash

Cash popup → **Add cash**. Send USDG to your address **on Robinhood Chain only**. Any other token or network won't arrive.

## Move cash into Turbo

Cash popup → **To Turbo**. Your USDG crosses to Lighter through Relay in seconds.

```flow
Cash | USDG on Robinhood Chain | gold
Relay | USDG → USDC | white
Turbo cash | USDC on Lighter | pink
```

| | |
|---|---|
| Smallest move | $6 |
| Bridge cost | About 4¢ on $10, shown before you confirm |

**To Cash** runs the same path backwards and lands as USDG in your wallet.

## Cash out

Cash popup → **Cash out**. Pick an amount and a Robinhood Chain address. The network fee shows in dollars before you confirm and comes out of the amount, so no ETH needed. Sends can't be pulled back.
