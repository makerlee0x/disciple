# Cash and Turbo cash

Two balances, both in dollars.

```cards
Cash | USDG · Robinhood Chain | | | gold
Turbo cash | USDC · Lighter | | | cyan
```

| | Cash | Turbo cash |
|---|---|---|
| Used for | Launching and trading rockets, the Market | Duel and Climb |
| Lives in | Your wallet on Robinhood Chain | Your own account on Lighter |

## How money moves

```tree
Cash | USDG in your wallet | gold
Card · Apple Pay | In: $10 to $2,000 | easy
Send USDG | In: Robinhood Chain only | easy
To Turbo | Out to Turbo cash, from $6 | cyan
Cash out | Out to any address or @user | white
```

## Add cash

- **Card or Apple Pay:** pick an amount and pay. It lands as Cash in a few minutes. The card provider's fee shows before you pay.
- **Send USDG:** to your address, **on Robinhood Chain only**. Any other token or network won't arrive.

## Cash ⇄ Turbo cash

```flow
Cash | USDG · Robinhood Chain | gold
Relay | Swaps and bridges in seconds | white
Turbo cash | USDC · Lighter | cyan
```

| | |
|---|---|
| Smallest move | $6 |
| Cost | About 4¢ on $10, shown before you confirm |

**To Cash** runs the same path backwards and lands as USDG in your wallet.

## Cash out

Pick an amount and a Robinhood Chain address or a DISCIPLE `@user`. The network fee shows in dollars before you confirm and comes out of the amount, so no ETH needed. Sends can't be pulled back.
