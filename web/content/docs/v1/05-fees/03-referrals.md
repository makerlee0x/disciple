# Referrals

Invite friends with your link. When they trade, you earn a small share of the trade fee.

## Your link

Open **Rewards** (the chest on your profile). Your invite link is there, with a **Copy** button.

## How a friend becomes yours

1. Your friend opens your link.
2. They sign in.

The first link a friend opens is the one that counts. Opening someone else's link later doesn't change it. You can't refer yourself. [CONFIRM: FLOW — saving the first link to the account at sign-in is in progress.]

## What you earn

You earn 1% of the trade fee on your friends' trades. The trade fee is 1% of each trade, so your share is 0.01% of the trade. On a $100 trade, that's $0.01. On a $1,000 trade, it's $0.10.

## Getting paid

For now, referral earnings are paid out by hand. There's nothing to claim in the app.

Your **Rewards** popup shows your totals: what you've earned from friends, and how many friends you've invited.
