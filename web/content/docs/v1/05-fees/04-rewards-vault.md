# Community rewards

The community rewards wallet collects two things today:

- **Sniper tax from rockets that never reached orbit.** The sniper tax (paid on BOOSTs in a rocket's first 7 seconds) is held aside. If the rocket reaches orbit, it goes into the rocket's locked liquidity instead. If the rocket hasn't reached orbit 30 days after launch, anyone can send it here. It's held in that rocket's base coin.
- **The referral share of the trade fee** (1% of the 1% fee). Referral earnings are worked out from it and paid by hand. See [Referrals](/docs/fees/referrals).

## How it's shared

Not decided yet. There are no automatic payout rules today. Payouts are made by the team, and every payout is a public transfer that anyone can look up. [CONFIRM: FLOW + lee — distribution rules and timing.]
