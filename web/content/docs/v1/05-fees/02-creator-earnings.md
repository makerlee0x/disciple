# Creator earnings

As a creator you earn from every trade on your rocket.

| Source | Size | When |
|---|---|---|
| Creator share of the trade fee | 33% of the 1% fee | Every BOOST and DUMP, before and after graduation |
| Creator tax | 0–7% of the trade, if you set one | Bonding-curve trades only |

On a $100 trade that's $0.33 from the fee, plus your creator tax on the curve.

## Claiming

Earnings build up on-chain and you claim them anytime, from your rocket or profile. There's no deadline and no minimum. [CONFIRM: SITE — where the claim button lives in the app.]

## If you were referred

If a referrer brought you to DISCIPLE, they get 2% of your 33% creator share on rockets you launch after your referral locked in. You keep the other 98%. See [Referrals](/docs/fees/referrals).

## Transfers

If you hand off your rocket, the right to claim future earnings goes with it. [CONFIRM: FLOW — whether unclaimed earnings move with the transfer or stay claimable by the previous creator.]
