# Flight and altitude

Open any rocket to fly it. Height is mcap. Every BOOST is thrust, every DUMP is a bomb, and up to 12 other pilots fly next to you as wingmen.

## Live flights

The list of live rockets. Sort by **Newest · Trending · Volume · Near orbit · Ascended** and filter by difficulty. A blue check means the team verified who's behind the rocket.
