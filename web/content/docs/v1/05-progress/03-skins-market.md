# Skins, crates and Market

Every skin is one hull in one paint. Win crates, open them, fly what you pull or sell it on the Market. Cosmetic only: skins never change price, fills or odds.

## Getting crates

| Crate | How |
|---|---|
| Uncommon | Win a ranked duel |
| Rare | 3 ranked wins in a row |
| Epic | 5+ in a row, or finish all weekly Orders |
| Rare (welcome) | Your first win, bot duels included. Once. |

Up to 15 duel crates a week. Never for sale. Levels and trophies also unlock reward skins and trails. Reward and welcome items can't be traded.

Each copy also rolls a burn grade (Factory Fresh → Burnt Out), a pattern, and sometimes FlightTrak (counts your ranked wins) or First edition.

## Market

Buy and sell skins for USDG. Equip what you own under **Your rocket** in your Hangar.

| | |
|---|---|
| Market fee | 7% of the sale |
| Network fee | Shown before you buy |
