# Duel

1v1 on live BTC. Same fuel, same price, one minute. Most PnL at the buzzer wins.

```steps
Ready up | 3 · 2 · 1, then the clock runs.
Long or short | BOOST longs, DUMP shorts. 50× on.
Out-PnL them | Bigger PnL at 0:00 wins.
```

## The round

```split
3.5 | Countdown | 3 · 2 · 1 | white
60 | The race | 60s | gold
30 | Overtime, if it's close | +30s | pink
```

## Controls

```keys
1 2 3 4 | Size: 10% · 25% · 50% · ALL of the fuel left | white
Space | BOOST: long | up
Shift | DUMP: short | down
Enter | Cash out: close it all | gold
W A S D | Steer (or arrows) | white
```

Same side again adds to your position at a new average entry. No flips: cash out first, then go the other way. No partial closes.

Ranked queues use the three tiers. Friend duels set any fuel from $5 up.

## The rules

- **NUKE:** at 50× a position that loses 39% of its fuel (a 0.78% BTC move) is closed. Real-money duels need Turbo cash of 1.2× the fuel ($24 for a $20 duel).
- **Overtime:** within 25¢ per $20 of fuel at the buzzer, you get 30 more seconds. Once.
- **Win:** beat your rival's PnL. You each keep your own PnL. Nobody pays the winner.
