# What is DISCIPLE

DISCIPLE is a launchpad and a rocket game on Robinhood Chain.

Everything launched here is a **rocket**. Every rocket is paired with a coin you believe in, its **base**. The rocket is priced and traded in that base.

The chart is the sky. **Altitude** is how high a rocket's value has climbed. The game shows that climb as named tiers, such as Morning Stars. Buying is **BOOST**: it lifts the rocket. Selling is **DUMP**: it drops a bomb, sized by how hard the sell moves the price.

## How a rocket's life works

![A rocket's life: it launches at $3,333, climbs its starting price path, reaches orbit at $33,333 when about 84% has been bought, then keeps flying on the open market with its liquidity locked forever.](/illustrations/docs/rocket-life.svg)

1. **Launch.** Anyone can launch a rocket. Give it a name, a ticker and a picture, and pick a difficulty.
2. **Climb the starting price path.** The rocket starts at a value of $3,333. People also call this path a bonding curve, which means each buy costs a little more than the last. Every BOOST and DUMP trades against that path.
3. **Reach orbit.** When about 84% of the rocket has been bought, at a value of $33,333, the rocket reaches orbit. That means the starting price path is finished. In the same trade, its liquidity moves to the open market on Uniswap and is locked forever in the Uniswap v4 LP.
4. **Keep flying.** After orbit the rocket trades on the open market on Uniswap. The climb continues through 18 named tiers, up to Seventh Heaven. Altitude is that climb.

## What makes it different

- **Paired with a coin, not ETH.** Every rocket is paired with a base coin, picked from 12 at launch. STRATEGY is the default.
- **One fee, split four ways.** Every BOOST and DUMP pays 1%. The creator, the rocket's locked liquidity and the treasury each get 33%, and the remaining 1% is a separate share. There's no gas to pay on trades.
- **Liquidity locked forever.** Once a rocket reaches orbit, its liquidity is locked forever in the Uniswap v4 LP. Nobody can withdraw it.
- **A game on top of the market.** Flight is the screen for one rocket. Bombs, crews and missions are part of that screen. Game features never change the price, the trade, or the odds.
