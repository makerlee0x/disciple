# What is DISCIPLE

DISCIPLE is a launchpad and a rocket game on Robinhood Chain.

Every coin launched here is a **rocket**. Every rocket is paired with a coin you believe in, its **base**. The rocket is priced and traded in that base.

The chart is the sky. A rocket's market cap is its **altitude**. Buying is **BOOST**: it lifts the rocket. Selling is **DUMP**: it drops a bomb, sized by how hard the sell moves the price.

## How a rocket's life works

1. **Launch.** Anyone can launch a rocket. Pick a base, a name, a call sign and an image.
2. **Climb the curve.** The rocket starts at a $3,333 market cap on a bonding curve. Every BOOST and DUMP trades against the curve.
3. **Graduate.** When 84% of supply has sold, at $33,333, the rocket graduates. In the same transaction, its liquidity moves into a Uniswap v4 pool and is locked forever.
4. **Keep flying.** After graduation the rocket trades on Uniswap v4. The climb continues through 18 altitude tiers, up to Seventh Heaven.

## What makes it different

- **Pair with a coin you believe in.** A rocket is not paired with ETH by default. It is paired with a base you choose. STRATEGY is the default, USDG is always available.
- **One fee, split four ways.** Every BOOST and DUMP pays 1%. The creator, the rocket's locked liquidity, the treasury and referrers share it.
- **Liquidity locked forever.** Graduated liquidity sits in a contract with no function to remove it.
- **A game on top of the market.** Flight view, bombs, crews, achievements and identities. Game features never change price, fills or odds.

> **Status.** DISCIPLE runs on Robinhood Chain Testnet today, with test tokens. Mainnet isn't live yet, and there's no date. See [Testnet](/docs/testnet).
