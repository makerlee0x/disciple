# Quick start

Five minutes from zero to your first BOOST.

## 1. Sign in

Open the menu and tap **Sign in**. Sign in with X, email or a wallet. Sign in once and DISCIPLE creates your account and wallet. After that, every trade is one tap, with no wallet pop-ups. See [Sign in and your account](/docs/account).

## 2. Deposit

Open **Wallet** and tap **Deposit**. Copy your account address and send money to it.

On the test network, the Deposit screen also has **Get free test money**. That money is only for trying the site and has no real value.

See [Deposits, withdrawals and gas](/docs/funds).

## 3. Pick a rocket

The **Launchpad** lists rockets. Open one to fly it: the chart becomes the sky, and the rocket's altitude is its market cap.

## 4. BOOST

Pick an amount and press **BOOST** (or `Space`). You pay in the rocket's base. The rocket lifts.

## 5. DUMP when you want out

Pick a share of your bag (10%, 25%, 50% or 100%) and press **DUMP** (or `Shift`). You're paid in the base. Your sell drops a bomb on the chart.

## Or launch your own

Press **Launch a Rocket**. Choose a base, a name, a call sign and an image. See [Launch a rocket](/docs/launch).

> Everything on testnet is for testing. Don't send real funds to a testnet address.
