## What these terms cover

These terms apply when you use DISCIPLE. That covers the website, the game, and the tools for launching and trading rockets on Robinhood Chain.

A rocket is a new coin. Buying a rocket is called BOOST. Selling a rocket is called DUMP.

By signing in, or by using DISCIPLE, you agree to these terms. If you do not agree, do not use the site.

## Signing in

Sign in with email, Google, X or wallet. Sign-in is handled by Privy.

If you sign in with email, Google, or X, Privy creates a wallet inside the app for you. If you sign in with a wallet you already have, that wallet is only how you log in, and Privy still creates one wallet inside the app for you. That in-app wallet controls an account built with Alchemy's tools. The account pays the network fee in USDG, the token DISCIPLE uses for trades, so a normal trade does not ask you for ETH.

One-tap trading keeps an expiring permission in your browser, so a later BOOST or DUMP can be sent without asking you to approve every tap.

To BOOST an Easy rocket, you need to be signed in with email, Google, or X. Signing in with a wallet alone does not pass that check. You can still DUMP without it. Easy, Hard, and Trench are the three modes a launcher picks when they create a rocket. The mode is fixed at launch.

You are responsible for your account, your devices, and anything done with them. Keep your login, and any recovery details you hold, private. We will never ask for your seed phrase, recovery phrase, or private keys.

## Anyone can launch a rocket

Anyone can launch a rocket. The rocket is priced in a token DISCIPLE supports. That token is a coin that wallets already know how to hold, and it is the coin you use to buy and sell the rocket.

We do not create other people's rockets, and we do not endorse them or vouch for what they claim. The name, the ticker, the picture, the links, and the claims come from the person who launched it. A ticker is the short capital-letter name a rocket trades under.

If you launch a rocket, you are responsible for what it is called, what it says, and whether it breaks a law or someone else's rights.

We may hide a rocket, a picture, or a profile on the website, for example if it is illegal, copies someone else's work, or is hateful or sexually explicit. We cannot change or delete a record that Robinhood Chain has already confirmed. That chain is a public ledger. Anyone can read it, and a confirmed record stays as it was written.

## A confirmed trade stays confirmed

Launches, BOOST, DUMP, and every other action that Robinhood Chain confirms are final. We cannot reverse them, refund them, or cancel them. The price can move between the moment you tap and the moment the trade lands.

At first, a rocket trades on its own market. A BOOST pushes the price up. A DUMP pushes the price down. When a rocket grows enough to leave that first market, its liquidity is locked forever in the Uniswap v4 LP. A liquidity pool (an LP) is the shared pile of tokens people trade against. Nobody can withdraw that locked pile, including us. People can still sell, and the price can still fall a long way.

## Fees

Every BOOST and every DUMP pays a 1% fee.

The person who launches a rocket can set a creator tax from 0% up to 7%. They choose it on the create page, and it does not change after launch. While the rocket is on its first market, that tax is taken from buys and sells on top of the 1% fee.

On an Easy rocket, while it is still on its first market, a sell can also pay a crew tip of 0.777% of the amount that sell would pay you. The tip applies only when the profit on that sale is above a threshold set for that rocket. Hard and Trench do not charge this tip.

In the first few seconds after a launch, a BOOST pays an extra tax that starts high and falls to nothing. The launcher's own first buy does not pay that extra tax.

Network fees on trades are paid in USDG, from a balance kept for that purpose. A normal trade does not ask you for ETH. The network fee can change.
