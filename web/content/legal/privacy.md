# Privacy Policy

This page says what DISCIPLE can see about you, and what stays public because it is written on a blockchain. A blockchain is a public ledger. Robinhood Chain is the one DISCIPLE uses. The other pages are the [Terms of Use](/terms) and the [Risk](/risk) page.

## The short version

We collect what we need to sign you in, to run the game, and to slow down abuse. Rockets, buys, sells, and wallet addresses on Robinhood Chain are public. Anyone can see them, and we cannot delete them.

## When you sign in

Sign in with email, Google, X or wallet. Sign-in is handled by Privy. Privy publishes its own privacy policy, and that policy applies to the data Privy handles.

If you use email, Privy sends a sign-in code to that address, and we receive the address.

If you use Google, we receive the email address on that Google account. We do not get your Google password.

If you use X, we can tell that the login was an X account. That is enough for the Easy buy check described below. We do not get your X password.

## A wallet you already have

If you sign in with a wallet you already have, we see its public address and the messages you sign to log in and to approve actions. We never ask for its private keys or its seed phrase. The wallet app you use has its own privacy policy. That wallet is only the login. The next section describes the wallet the app then uses for trading.

## The wallet inside the app

Whichever way you sign in, Privy gives you one wallet inside the app. If that login already has one, Privy uses that one and does not make a second. Its public address shows on Robinhood Chain and inside DISCIPLE.

That in-app wallet controls an account built with Alchemy's tools. The account pays network fees in USDG, the token DISCIPLE uses for trades. One-tap trading keeps an expiring permission in this browser's storage, so a later trade can be sent without a fresh approval each time.

## Buying an Easy rocket

Buying is called BOOST. Selling is called DUMP. To BOOST an Easy rocket, you need a sign-in with email, Google, or X. A wallet-only sign-in does not pass that check. DUMP does not need that check. A note that you passed is written on Robinhood Chain for a limited time, so the rocket can see it.

## What everyone can see

Rockets, BOOST trades, DUMP trades, launches, balances, and wallet addresses are recorded on Robinhood Chain. That record is public, and it stays there. Anyone can read it. We cannot edit it or delete it. DISCIPLE shows it in feeds, in holder lists, on leaderboards, and next to names.

## Your profile

You can add a username, a short bio, and a profile picture. On a rocket you launch, you can also add an X handle or a website link. These are public on your profile and next to your name. You can change them or clear them. You can change your username once every 24 hours.

Your profile also shows figures worked out from trades that are already public: profit and loss, win rate, and bombs dropped. Bombs dropped is a game count of sells that moved the price. Anyone who opens your profile can see these figures.

## What we store so the site can run

Messages you post in a rocket's chat can be read by anyone looking at that rocket.

Pictures you upload are checked for sexual content before we use them. Your browser runs a first check with a picture model that ships with the site. Our server then runs the check we rely on. That server check can be Google Cloud Vision, Amazon Rekognition, Sightengine, or a picture model running on the server. Which one runs depends on how the site is configured.

We use your IP address to limit repeated requests.

Usernames, a list of trades, chat messages, and pictures that passed the check are stored in Upstash, a hosted database. The app is built to be hosted on Vercel.

## A cookie the site sets

If you open a referral link, we store a cookie in your browser for 30 days so the person who referred you can be credited. The cookie holds that person's username or wallet address, and the time you arrived. We also copy that same note into this browser's storage.

## Analytics

The app does not include a separate analytics tool.

## Your choices

You can edit your profile, and you can sign out.

## Children

DISCIPLE is not for anyone under 18.

## If this policy changes

If we change this policy, we will post the new one on this page.
