# Support

Email [hello@disciple.vip](mailto:hello@disciple.vip). The Team reads every message.

You can also send a DM to [@disciplevip](https://x.com/disciplevip) on X.

## What to include

Your username, the coin or duel, and the time it happened. For a trade or a transfer, add the transaction link. We never ask for your private key, your seed phrase, or a sign-in code. Anyone who asks for one is not the Team.

## Report a player or a coin

Email the username or the coin page link and what happened. The Team reviews every report and removes content or accounts that break the [Terms of Use](/terms).

## Delete your account

Settings → Security → Delete account, in the app or on the website. Trades and coins already on Robinhood Chain stay public, because the chain can't be edited.
